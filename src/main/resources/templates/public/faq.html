Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to common questions regarding ordering, Udhari facility, GST billing, and farming advisory

You can browse our online catalog, add your required products to the shopping cart, and proceed to checkout. You can select either Counter Pickup or Field Delivery, and pay via Cash, UPI QR code, or Udhari.

Verified local farmers with an active customer account can choose Udhari at checkout. The bill amount is automatically logged to your personal credit ledger with a 30-day payment term. You can view your balance and pay online via UPI or cash anytime.

Log in to your Customer Dashboard, go to My Orders, click on your order, and select Download / Print GST Invoice. The system generates a compliant invoice with HSN and tax breakdown.

The calculator uses standard state agricultural university (MPKV Rahuri / ICAR) fertilizer recommendations based on acreage and soil type. For micro-soil deficiencies, farmers are encouraged to also review their soil health cards.

Our physical retail counter is open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:30 PM, and on Sundays from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Online orders and inquiries can be submitted 24/7.
