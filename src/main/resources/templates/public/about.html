आम्ही Mahyco, Syngenta, IFFCO, Bayer CropScience, Jain Irrigation यांसारख्या देशातील आघाडीच्या कंपन्यांचे अधिकृत वितरक आहोत. प्रत्येक उत्पादन १००% प्रमाणित आणि खात्रीशीर आहे.

Saptashrungi Agro Services is an authorized agricultural input and advisory center in Mirakhnagar, Vaijapur. Our dedication is to provide local farmers with superior quality seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and modern equipment at fair and transparent prices.

We are authorized distributors for India's leading manufacturers including Mahyco, Syngenta, IFFCO, Bayer CropScience, and Jain Irrigation. Every single product is 100% genuine and batch-certified.

Expert Agronomist Guidance

Personalized consultation on seed varieties, fertilizer dosages, and pest spraying schedules.

Reliable seasonal credit facility for verified local farmers with convenient harvest repayment.
